A New Canopy Begins to Grow

The Indian Valley Chamber was proud to recognize Michaela "Mickey" Trammell, CEO of the Dixie Fire Canopy Project, as the 2026 Gold Diggers Day Grand Marshal.

Mickey also celebrated the Dixie Fire Canopy Project's acquisition of the greenhouse at The Spot in downtown Greenville, cutting the ribbon to open the organization's new nursery. Already beginning to fill, the greenhouse is bringing new green back to the heart of our community.

07 18 2026

Gold Diggers 2026

Gold Diggers may not look the way many of us remember it, but gathering together each summer remains one of Greenville's most meaningful traditions.

The celebration has taken on new meaning since the Dixie Fire, standing as a symbol of resilience, perseverance, and our community's determination to keep moving forward.

Thank you to everyone who joined us this year. We can't wait to celebrate with you again.

IVCC Welcomes Coyote Karma

We’re excited to welcome Coyote Karma to Crescent Mills, where they’ll be opening inside the former Crescent Country building. Their collection of vintage finds, antiques, gifts, oddities, and online sales promises a unique shopping experience for locals and visitors alike.

Owners Valery and Patrick are making the move from Taos, New Mexico, bringing a fresh perspective while building on a location that has long been familiar to our community. We can’t wait to see what treasures await inside.
